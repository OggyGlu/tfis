Campus Academic Tracks

Our curriculum provides a rigorous, international framework designed to balance mathematical logic, linguistic precision, and critical scientific research skills. We aim to guide students toward structured independent thinking, cross-disciplinary problem solving, and global cultural empathy.

By mapping out our core layout classes into two distinct structural stages, we smoothly transition your child from foundational childhood literacy up to high-performing secondary readiness:

Primary School Division

Grade 1 – Grade 5 (Ages 6 – 10)

Focuses on building high-performance fluency in core reading comprehension, conceptual arithmetic, and structural scientific observation. Students step forward into classroom environments where collaborative group research assignments and evidence-based writing modules are introduced natively.

English Phonics & Literacy Foundational Mathematics General Sciences Social Studies & Cultures Creative Arts & Crafts

Middle School Division

Grade 6 – Grade 8 (Ages 11 – 13)

Introduces specialized subject divisions to maximize critical analysis and technical problem solving before students enter secondary testing paths. Course tracks place a premium on independent research report writing, complex algebraic algorithms, empirical science laboratory cycles, and formal presentation debates.

Advanced English Literature Pre-Algebra & Geometry Basics Integrated Physics & Chemistry World History & Geography Intro to Computer Coding

Comprehensive Development Metrics

Beyond standard text tracking, Grade 1 to Grade 8 students actively participate in weekly laboratory cycles, physical educational games, musical rehearsals, and global citizenship discussions. This guarantees our scholars grow with a completely balanced approach to mind, character, and physical fitness.
