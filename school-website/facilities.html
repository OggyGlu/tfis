Our Campus Facilities

First International School balances modern classrooms with comprehensive athletic venues and scientific laboratories to guarantee a world-class environment for student discovery.

Advanced Science Laboratories

Equipped with modern chemistry, biology, and physics apparatus. Our lab tracks encourage students to run advanced empirical experiments under strict security baselines.

Digital Innovation & Computing Labs

Featuring modern high-performance desktop terminals. Students utilize this space for computational workflows, office software training, and digital design electives.

Central Research Library

A quiet media center containing an extensive catalogue of international textbooks, research materials, fiction, and open digital access terminals for global research.

Interactive Smart Classrooms

Every classroom incorporates bright multi-media projection systems, modern climate control systems, and ergonomic seating arrays to optimize learning concentration.

Indoor & Outdoor Athletics Complex

Our expansive recreational zones feature multipurpose indoor basketball courts, professional outdoor football greens, and dedicated early-years safe play fields.

Creative Arts & Music Studios

A vibrant, open studio zone configured for painting, sculpture, and musical instrument practice. Includes specialized acoustic treatment zones for group rehearsals.
