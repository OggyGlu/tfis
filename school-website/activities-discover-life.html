Discover Life Program

After School Clubs Discover Life Program

Experiential Learning Beyond Borders

Our Discover Life program is the cornerstone of holistic education at First International School. Designed specifically for Grade 1 through Grade 8 students, this experiential framework drives learning out of classroom rows and hooks it straight into practical cultural landscapes, community fields, and nature tracking modules.

By interacting directly with real-world environments, our scholars cultivate deep personal independence, social accountability, and true global citizenship foundations.

Program Tracks

Educational Field Excursions

Termly Expeditions

Curated trips mapping to historical sites, science museums, and botanical nature parks. Students gather research data firsthand to feed into their main academic portfolios.

Annual Teambuilding Camps

Annual Overnights

Outdoor camps focusing on navigation coordinates, group tent pitching, hiking safety tracks, and cooperative leadership drills that challenge personal boundaries.

Civic Service & Engagement

Ongoing Projects

Fosters global citizenship by partnering with localized neighborhood drives. Students organize recycling networks, plant trees, and lead book-sharing programs.
