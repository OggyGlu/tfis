Preschool & Foundation Levels

Our Early Years Foundation framework is structured to spark curiosity, encourage discovery, and cultivate a lifelong love for learning. We combine safe, play-based exploration with supportive sensory environments designed precisely for young scholars.

By mapping out explicit developmental milestones across three dedicated class tiers, our childhood educators ensure every student progresses naturally at a comfortable, confident rhythm.

Our Classroom Levels

We divide our early foundation cycles into three focused age tiers to target specific social, linguistic, and motor milestones:

Nursery

Age: 3 - 4 Years

Focuses heavily on sensory play, early language development, and essential socialization milestones. Children build motor skills through fun tactile exercises, collaborative storytelling circles, and guided parallel play.

Pre-KG

Age: 4 - 5 Years

Introduces structured foundation skills including phonics recognition, basic mathematical sorting, and structural pattern shapes. Emphasis is placed on building emotional independence, sharing with peers, and practicing clean personal care habits.

KG

Age: 5 - 6 Years

Prepares senior preschool students for smooth transitions into elementary school benchmarks. Curriculums introduce fundamental reading readiness, handwriting mechanics, advanced puzzle logic, and organized collaborative playground sports.

Safe, Inspiring Spaces

Our foundation classrooms utilize friendly color palates, child-safe rounded furniture configurations, and premium non-toxic creative supply kits. All indoor interactive spaces and outdoor playgrounds are constantly monitored under strict teacher supervision vectors to ensure your child's safety.
