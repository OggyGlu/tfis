Our Educational Foundations

The First International School operates under a clear framework dedicated to cultural understanding, critical academic analysis, and ethical local leadership development.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence that empowers students to lead with excellence, innovate with purpose and serve with social responsibility in a rapidly changing world

Our Mission

To provide a globally recognized education that nurtures curiosity, creativity, and character, empowering learners to lead as responsible global citizens

Our 3G Core Values

Our 3G values represent the foundational traits and operational competencies we expect every student to master during their tenure at First International School:

G1

Genuine

Nurturing honesty, moral authenticity, and true transparency in character. Our students learn to stay sincere in their intentions, hold true to ethical boundaries, and build authentic connections within our global community.

G2

Grit

Cultivating unyielding resilience, work ethic, and passion through academic hurdles. Scholars approach complex scientific questions, mathematical structures, and competitive tracks with a determination that refuses to give up.

G3

Gratitude

Instilling a profound sense of thankfulness, respect, and global appreciation. Students learn to celebrate their learning achievements, respect their heritage, and step forward as leaders who seek to serve their community with humility.

Our School

Providing premium international education options that inspire structural knowledge development, critical analytics, and strong citizenship paths.
