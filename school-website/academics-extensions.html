📍 Campus Location Notice

Please note that our IGCSE preparation modules and GED testing paths are managed and held exclusively at our specialized FIS Extension Campus (City Center), separate from the primary international school campus facilities.

Secondary & High School Equivalency Tracks

We provide comprehensive accelerated examination preparation courses to guide senior students toward globally recognized qualifications, ensuring direct pathways into top local and international universities.

Cambridge IGCSE Programme

The International General Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE) is the world's most popular international qualification for secondary school students. Our dedicated curriculum focuses on depth of knowledge, logical analysis, and global context mastery.

  • Core Subjects Offered: Extended Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Business Studies, and English as a First/Second Language.
  • Target Grade: Designed primarily for scholars aged 14 to 16.
  • Accreditation: Fully evaluated by Cambridge Assessment International Education frameworks.

GED Test Preparation Path

The General Educational Development (GED) credential provides a flexible high school equivalency option recognized by universities globally. Our extension campus track provides intensive boot camps tailored for rapid skill mastery.

  • Core Assessment Modules: Mathematical Reasoning, Science Analysis, Social Studies, and Reasoning Through Language Arts.
  • Path Design: Fast-track modules featuring daily interactive problem-solving mock canvas tests.
  • Flexible Intake: Year-round flexible schedules customized to matching individual learning speed preferences.
