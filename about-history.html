The History of First International School

Founded on a commitment to international academic achievement, our institution has grown to become an educational beacon in the region, bridging localized talent pools with prestigious global universities.

From our initial intake of primary scholars to our expansive secondary divisions today, we have continually upgraded our physical labs, localized teacher qualification criteria, and technical stack to support world-class discovery pathways.

Milestones & Evolution

2016

The Founding Vision

First International School opens its initial campus doors with a commitment to providing high-quality, international-standard education. The school begins with an enrollment of 45 preschool and early elementary students.

2019

Secondary Division Expansion

To support growing communities, the school commissions its secondary block, introducing multi-disciplinary computing, modern physics labs, and resource-heavy media centers.

2022

International Program Integration

The academic council aligns the curriculum tracks with Cambridge Assessments International Education benchmarks and introduces Advanced Placement (AP) frameworks for high school divisions.

2025 - Present

Global Accreditation Candidacy

The school formally enters active candidacy alignment status with the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC). Today, our diverse student population spans over multi-disciplinary paths preparing for leading global spaces.
