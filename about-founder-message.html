Message from our Founder & CEO

The First International School was established with a singular, clear core mission: to provide an uncompromising world-class learning space that empowers localized student communities to achieve competitive global success.

Sai Thiha Aung

Founder & Chief Executive Officer
"True education matches rigorous academic grit with an authentic moral compass. We don't just train students to pass tests; we cultivate global leaders who act with gratitude and lead with absolute integrity."

When we laid down our initial foundation parameters in 2016, our goal was to bridge the gap between regional potential and prestigious global university placements. By bringing together exceptional certified educators, international laboratory apparatus, and a safe, inquiry-driven school culture, we have created an ecosystem where students maximize their focus and performance daily.

We invite you to join our journey as we continue expanding our academic tracks, localized facility investments, and cross-disciplinary enrichment programs to prepare tomorrow's visionaries.
