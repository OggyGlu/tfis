After School Clubs

After School Clubs Discover Life Program

Extracurricular Enrichment tracks

At First International School, learning expands far beyond the final classroom period. Our After School Clubs program provides interactive environments where Grade 1 through Grade 8 students explore practical hobbies, build collaborative team habits, and discover localized creative pursuits.

Clubs run directly under senior faculty supervision vectors across specialized fields, encouraging every student to unlock unique alternative milestones.

Active Club Communities

Robotics & Engineering

Tuesdays & Thursdays | 4:00 PM

Students build custom robotic frames, study elementary hardware wiring logic, and code sensor paths using easy-to-learn computing block scripts.

Lions Football Club

Mondays & Wednesdays | 4:00 PM

Focuses on agility drills, game tactics, physical health training, and team play fields. Prepares athletes for clean competitive local friendly matches.

Forensic Debate & Model UN

Fridays | 3:30 PM

Empowers senior students to compile structured arguments, study intercultural geopolitics, practice advanced diction, and build poise on stage.

Green Ecology & Gardening

Wednesdays | 4:00 PM

Hands-on cultivation projects centered around localized plant botanical life, community organic composting loops, and recycling awareness programs.
