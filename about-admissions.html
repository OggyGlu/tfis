Enrollment Criteria & Process

Welcome to the First International School admissions hub. We accept applications year-round from local and international students who seek a comprehensive, cross-disciplinary global academic curriculum.

The Application Steps

Step 1: Application Inquiry

Parents submit an official inquiry or campus tour reservation form through our administrative registrar office via phone or direct administrative email.

Step 2: Documentation Submission

Provide previous school transcripts, student passport or birth certification records, and immunization health history records to our registrar desk.

Step 3: Placement Assessment

Prospective students take a standard grade-level evaluation covering baseline English literacy, text analytical skills, and mathematical logic structures.
