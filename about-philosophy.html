Our Educational Foundations

First International School operates under a clear framework dedicated to cultural understanding, critical academic analysis, and ethical local leadership development.

Our Vision

To foster an inclusive, globally integrated educational ecosystem where independent learners expand their individual potential to drive positive social development in an evolving 21st-century landscape.

Our Mission Statement

We deliver an intensive international curriculum path combining cross-disciplinary analytical skills with advanced digital literacy. By matching high expectations with custom student care plans, we empower scholars to graduate as compassionate, ethical global citizens.

Expected Schoolwide Learning Results (ESLRs)

Our ESLRs represent the foundational traits and operational competencies we expect every graduating student to master during their tenure at First International School:

E

Effective Communicators

Students will express concepts clearly, listen with empathy, and present structured academic writing across various local and international language platforms.

S

Strategic Thinkers & Problem Solvers

Students will utilize mathematical analysis, computational frameworks, and evidence-based logic to solve real-world community development puzzles.

L

Leaders with Integrity

Students will demonstrate deep moral commitment, respect collaborative group decisions, and accept accountability while working within diverse project tracks.

R

Responsible Global Citizens

Students will understand intercultural traditions, appreciate socioeconomic perspectives, and champion localized environmental conservation measures.
