Specialist Electives Program

Preschool (Nursery - KG) Grade 1 - 8 Core Education Electives Program IGCSE & GED Certification

Creative & Technical Specialist Pathways

Our electives program encourages students in Grades 1 to 8 to discover individual talents beyond the core textbook curriculum. These courses build practical technical fluencies, fine motor craft skills, and artistic confidence.

Information Technology & Coding

Students explore introductory block coding (Scratch), computer hardware setups, logical puzzle design, and basic keyboard office literacy to prepare for an evolving digital environment.

Visual Arts & Design Studio

Encourages creative self-expression through color theory, sketch molding, multi-material sculpting, and painting portfolios. Student masterpieces are proudly showcased in our gallery halls annually.

Music Performance & Theory

Introduces reading musical notations, classical instrument chords, and choral rhythm coordination tracks. Provides a supportive atmosphere for beginner instrumentalists and vocal groups.
